# MB4 · Where did the marks go?

promotion: discovery

## Goal (user's words)

"Here's the paper I sat, with the marks on it. Tell me what I lost the most marks on and get me practising that first."

## Definition of done

- They can enter the marked paper question by question on the phone, saying what each question tests, without knowing any spec codes.
- The TV shows the paper's total as they entered it, and the topics where the marks went, biggest loss first.
- What the desk cannot practise is listed apart and counted, never silently dropped and never forced onto the wrong topic.
- From that list they reach a practice set on the first topic in a few presses.
- Nothing claims an exam board, a grade or a pass the desk cannot back.

## Levels

- **L1** (walker over code): the phone's Paper panel and its validation, the `paper` screen, the way back to Get ready for school, and the set from there. Read from `desk/src/app/phone/PaperPanel.tsx`, `desk/src/lib/rules/paperEntry.ts`, `desk/src/lib/rules/recovery.ts`, `desk/src/lib/library/gcse.ts`, `desk/src/tv/paperRows.ts`, `desk/src/maths/MathsTV.tsx`, `desk/src/tv/keys.ts`.
- **L2** (not blocked on vision): entry is typed, so this journey needs **no vision call** today, and L2 could run it live as soon as an L2 driver exists. The photo of a marked paper (`desk/src/lib/desk/paperRead.ts`, vision on local Ollama with the qwen 27B model) has no caller and is not offered, so it is out of this journey until a Snap door ships. The practice it leads to is MB2's.
