# Campaign art rework 2 A — the league built from its victims

Owner authority: backlog N4 and the executing CAMPAIGN ART REWORK 2 instruction. The five technical themes remain industrial, quarry, desert, wetland and alpine. The campaign-beats.csv hubs are The Yards, Foundry row, The flats and quarry, The mountain workshop and The Crown pits (Salt Run, Switchback and Crown in course/cup terms); a hub spans courses, so it is not a sixth texture biome. Rust and Ink remains the prop/surface/vehicle language; Soot Pulp barriers and backdrops remain intact.

The Ash Circuit occupies a burnt-out rust-belt transport economy. People keep workshops alive by reclaiming parts. Marrow builds collection gates, welded start gantries, impound bays and propaganda hoardings from the same scrap he takes as collateral. Nothing looks factory-new. Race income buys fleet steel while public infrastructure rots. Original league slogans: “THE TRACK COLLECTS”, “YOUR DEBT. OUR FINISH.” and “WIN THE HEAT. KEEP THE RECEIPT.” Blank generated sign faces receive deterministic lettering from the existing game font during scenery baking; real brands never appear.
