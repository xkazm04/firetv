# EM5 · My writing, kept and known

promotion: discovery

## Goal (user's words, an adult)

"Keep a few of my own emails, tell me what makes them long or unclear, and show me how I write, without putting my clients' business on the TV."

## Definition of done

- An adult keeps a few of their own messages or emails from the PC and sees them listed on the TV by title, never by content.
- They get a kept piece read through a lens and judge the advice fair for that kind of writing.
- After enough pieces of one kind, the desk describes how they write in words they recognise, and they can take that description away.
- They can remove any piece, or everything they kept, and see it gone.

## Levels

- **L1 (always).** A walker over Adult mode's entrance, the Workroom, the PC page, the shelf, the twin's birth rule and the Twin Card's Adult gate.
- **L2: not covered by `tools/essay-ui-test.cjs`.** The harness seats a Family learner (high-school, 15) and never opens `/drop`, the Workroom or `/api/twin/card`. An L2 for this journey needs a new script: an `other` profile with Mode Adult, three pieces kept on `/drop`, the Workroom on the TV with its text checked for any kept sentence, the card downloaded and checked against the vendored schema.
