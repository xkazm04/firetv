Decisions for owner review

No choices are preselected. A Keep is a design preference; it does not override a failed measurement. Notes stay in this browser until exported.

01 · Seven visits with a purpose

Proposed default: retain 35 events and every course. Four build-up visits lead to a pressure run; a shorter qualifier gives room to prepare; the boss contest ends in a saved payout and a consequence beat.

Each act has a distinct job: shelter → receipts → route → public evidence → freedom. 753 old laps become 367. Stable event IDs preserve saves.

02 · Beat the named boss

Proposed default: finish alive ahead of the named boss to earn promotion. Other entrants still determine prize money and cup points. Third place can recruit a fourth-place boss.

A wreck or unfinished attempt cannot promote. No loss counter grants victory. The finale still requires being the last car running.

03 · A finite power ladder

Proposed default: keep global ceiling 10 and three weapon slots; preserve class weaknesses with shared speed/grip limits. Early tiers are acquisition; Pro completes a build; Elite and Champion provide different strengths inside a finite physical envelope.

Explicit owner decision: replace the impossible late 0.89 PR target with a 0.93–1.03 band and judge tension through named-boss outcomes. The old-band misses remain visible. Line speed is capped at 8.125; Bastion grip at 9; Quill and Kestrel speed at 9.5. Stock Line speed is 5.5 instead of 6 and handling is 3 instead of 4; its original launch is retained. Installed tiers stay saved; their derived stats follow the shared limits. Physics and visible offers use the same fractional values. The measured gates below decide whether this candidate is balanced.

04 · Promotion through allies

Proposed default: choose money; the named car or its missing tier peer; or the ally specialty part with a useful unlocked fallback. The exact asset is named before claiming.

Money remains 350 / 650 / 1,000 / 1,400 CR. Options have different market values. A complete garage may leave only cash useful; pending choices survive restart. Allies give no hidden race boost.

05 · The receipts return real money

Proposed default: Ox proves that diverted payments bought the enforcement fleet. Recovery reduces remaining principal first; the rest returns as cash. A full wallet retains the unpaid amount.

Debt never gates entry and insured repairs preserve a usable car. Marrow later invents an ownership lien even when the debt is zero. Seizure exposes his demand for control rather than a new surprise bill.

06 · A coalition with something to lose

Proposed default: Rook opens the crew account; Ox brings duplicate receipts; Vex opens the courier road and releases the recording; Mica distributes evidence and wins public access to the Crown.

The young Mechanic hides the sibling and builds the quarry-dispatcher rig in view of the player. Secrecy comes from fear and protection; there is no random betrayal. Text deepens the plot without new art or voice recordings.

07 · The last car running

Proposed default: Marrow takes your developed car. The Mechanic supplies a basic Line rig with the automatic half-metre Mine Dispatcher. One-on-one; no lap win; free fully repaired retries.

Victory ends Marrow’s reign and returns the seized car. The rig is a fixed basic build on loan, with its original 6 / 3 / 5 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 2 / 4 stats shown in game; it is not a sellable reward. The crew coalition becomes the new league. The fight-to-the-death ending preserves the owner plot.

08 · A shorter complete campaign

Owner decision required: adopt a provisional 4–6 hour campaign target instead of the old 5–8 hours. The old race-informed Club median was 10.24 simulated driving hours with 233/667 careers still censored.

The new measured completed and censored hours are shown separately below. The earlier 3–5 hour proposal was too optimistic: final completed driving medians are about 4.60 hours for Club decisions and 3.73 for Pro. Adding an explicitly assumed 30–60 minutes for shops and story supports a typical 4–6 hour target; retry-heavy tails run longer. Neither proxy timing nor a Keep choice establishes human pacing or enjoyment.
