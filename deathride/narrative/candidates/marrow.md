# Candidates: Marrow's key lines

Generated from the blind judging run of 2026-10-04 (see `docs/narrative/WRITING-PROCESS.md`). Each slot was drafted as one pool in four constraint batches: **A** short (at most 8 words a line), **B** a concrete object or number required, **C** open on a verb with no adjectives, **D** an angle most writers would not take. Two judges (J1 and J2, different models, no access to drafts, authorship or batch letters) scored every candidate in independently shuffled orders against the R3 C1 rubric.

Score strings list the ten dimensions in order (A V S E Sp F Fr T Ear Tone), 1 to 5. **Mean** is the average of the two judges' means. **Pass** means the line meets R3's ship rule for that judge: an average of at least 4, no dimension below 3, and Voice and Tells at least 4. A round-2 column appears where a slot went through the targeted revision round (judges J3 and J4).
