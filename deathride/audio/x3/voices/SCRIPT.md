# Campaign voice script — text before synthesis

Source: deathride/campaign/OWNER-CAMPAIGN-CHOICE.md. Original adaptation; no Marrow impersonation. Announcer Callum (noir); Mechanic Harry (nervous, helpful shop owner).

The inherited game story is older than this owner choice. New seizure/ally/last-car-standing beats are prepared assets; they must only trigger once the corresponding campaign states exist. Do not describe the existing lap-based duel as elimination.

## announcer.debt

campaign opening: survival debt

> Your engine is collateral. Every race buys another day. Marrow keeps the books.

## announcer.boss

boss briefing

> Beat the division boss. Give them a reason to stand beside you.

## announcer.ally

boss turns ally; payout type undecided

> A beaten rival. A new ally. Their backing keeps you on the road.

## announcer.seizure

car seizure

> Marrow has taken your car. The debt was never meant to end.

## announcer.duel

death duel rules

> No laps. No points. The last car running ends this.

## announcer.freedom

final victory

> The ledger is closed. This road belongs to the living.

## mechanic.welcome

shop introduction

> You made it. Good. Let me check that engine before Marrow sends you out again.

## mechanic.repair

shop repair

> Easy. I can hold this together. Just bring it back in one piece, all right?

## mechanic.books

debt twist; no unsupported money destination

> I checked the receipts. You keep winning, but his numbers never let you go.

## mechanic.ally

ally reward types; no choice mechanic assumed

> That boss is backing you now. Money, a car, parts. We finally have some help.

## mechanic.seizure

seizure response

> He took your car? Right. Right... I thought he might. Come round the back.

## mechanic.rig

secret basic car and mine rig

> It is basic. I know. But I fitted a mine dispatcher. Keep moving and let him follow.

## mechanic.duel

duel setup

> No finish line this time. You come back. Marrow does not.

## mechanic.after

aftermath without promising seized car return

> You are back. I kept listening for the engine. Thought I had lost you.
