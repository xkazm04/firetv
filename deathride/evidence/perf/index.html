What changed and what remains

  • Baseline native audio slept on the render thread. The worker plus monitor-free mailboxes removes the measured dependency; native audio CPU p95 is 0.375 ms in the repeated trace.
  • HUD metadata deltas reduce roughly 25K characters per message to about 1.6K, with unchanged dynamic data and full refresh/reconnect behavior.
  • Static road marks match all 196,608 audited RGBA pixels and reduce mean render CPU by 1.335 ms in the matched-APK experiment.
  • Steady audio render allocation falls to zero in the targeted changing-engine test; onsets and other rendering still allocate. Android per-thread allocation is unavailable, so desktop JFR is explicitly a proxy.
  • Rejected packets cross the unchanged 250 ms age cutoff after shared receive gaps; sequence ordering and ordinary handler/ack-enqueue work do not explain the long delay.
  • Active texture uploads are zero; 720p did not improve p95. Native image quality and every effect are preserved.
  • A rejection-triggered native ring captures full 285 ms gaps with 17 render entries, no overlapping app GC and maximum app lock wait 0.047 ms. Kernel packet-arrival timestamps remain unavailable.
