# Campaign art rework 2 B — expression before costume

The owner keeps Soot Pulp and asks to see people, not their equipment. Seven portraits cover Rook, Ox, Vex, Mica, Relay, Marrow and the young Mechanic. Eight character panels cover debt, four allies, seizure, the rig reveal and the workshop ending. The overhead two-car finale has no character face and remains unchanged, as do ledger icons, debt meter and the approval-blocked rig state family.

Portraits frame a complete head and a narrow collar/shoulder hint. Forehead-to-chin facial height targets 50–60% of the whole square, with both eyes open and separated, a readable mouth and no goggles, mask or featureless shadow over the eyes. Shoulder armour is context. Cards use the same medium-close framing: the face carries the dramatic beat while receipts, clamps, covered cars and the mine rig become subordinate setting. Environment details come from part A's bible. Identity references remain the original portraits; Ox keeps the established female design despite inherited masculine campaign prose.

The face-visibility gate uses a hash-bound Qwen local-model observation of the exact exported frame and its native HUD/card preview. It supplies facial-oval and eye coordinates, framing, visibility, a single clear primary subject, native expression and a concrete defect list. Deterministic checks reject face height below 40%, area below 10%, clipped faces, invalid/too-close eyes, body-dominant framing, hidden features, unreadable expression, uncertainty and stale/missing evidence. Two local models separately grade source/style quality. Identity is inspected against the original portraits separately.

Calibration changed the draft detector selection, not its geometry thresholds. The initial three-image pilot confused the body-heavy identity reference with the target. MiMo's subsequent face boxes visibly missed facial features and called a measured 60% close-up small; single-image and nearest-pixel trials did not repair that. Its source/style observations remain, but it is not used as a face localizer. Qwen was checked against directly measured Rook/Marrow exports and the historical body-first Mechanic: the latter fails with a 28% model-measured face and 6px eye gap. All pilot observations remain archived. This is deterministic geometry over model localization, with a small calibration set, not a general accuracy claim. The models share Qwen architecture; direct inspection remains necessary and never grants owner approval.

The new face-first contract treats the face as the subject. A collar or shoulder may meet the image edge; the entire facial oval must remain inside a 2% frame margin. Legacy whole-sprite margin findings remain in `source_sprite_codes`, while all other pixel gates still apply. No car, environment or existing fusion gate is relaxed. Exact, unresampled identity crops become conditioning inputs after full-bust references repeatedly reproduce torso framing. Crop coordinates and parent/input hashes are audited; no crop is presented as newly generated art, and every candidate is measured over its complete final frame.

The gate also inverts the recorded export transform and checks the face against the original source frame: at least 40% source height and a 2% source margin. Transparent atlas gutters cannot disguise source clipping, and trimming broad empty margins cannot manufacture a size pass. A regression fixture covers both cases.

Rejected or unmeasured faces never enter the owner candidate gallery or candidate bundle. Exact source/export hashes bind all screening. The native previews use the actual smallest renderer sizes: 60px rival portraits and 112px career cards (the Mechanic uses a 70px box, and other cards are larger). These accompany enlarged before/after comparisons. Portrait exports use 128px cells; cards use 512px textures. Keep/Maybe/Reject and escaped Copy Markdown reuse the audio report interaction format. All owner flags remain false; generation proofs permit only bounded batch execution.

Story panels preserve the complete generated source with an aspect fit in a 512px soot canvas; they are never stretched or cropped to force a face-size pass. Face measurements include the entire final canvas. The debt proof's pale torn-paper border is explicitly disclosed for owner taste review. Its third attempt is the final allowed call for that logical item.
