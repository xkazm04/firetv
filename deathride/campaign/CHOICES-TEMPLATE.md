## The three answers that most change the build

1. **What does the player decide between races?** Select one primary structure: A's fixed five-act ladder; B's five hubs with two-event detours; C's episode heat order and sponsor choice; or D's persistent five-region map with two middle-region orders. For a fuse, name the one structure to retain and the systems to leave out. A traveling-garage story alone does not include route choice, and a territory story alone does not include a map.

Existing fusion art remains the visual direction; audio suggestions are untested text briefs.

3. **What scope and pacing boundary should the next build obey?** State whether to keep the current 35-event, 18/21/24-lap format for a first prototype or fund a shorter-event pacing experiment. Choose whether to accept the published boss-PR misses initially or fund the separate roster/handling balance work needed to pursue every 0.85–0.90 promotion dip. Confirm any M/L additions you actually want: route persistence, sponsor rules or territory ownership; physical escorts, point-to-point roads, live audience voting, team AI and real destruction derbies are excluded unless explicitly added.
