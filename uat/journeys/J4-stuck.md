# J4 · When I get stuck

promotion: discovery

## Goal (user's words)

"When I don't know what to say, help me say it — then let me try again on my own."

## Definition of done

- Stuck mid-conversation, the Character finds help (a cue, the phrase choice, or pausing to be coached) without leaving the scene.
- The help actually unblocks the next reply; a coaching note quotes what they really said.
- Replaying the moment gives them a fresh question, and they answer it better than before.
- Supported replies are not recorded as independent speaking (the progress picture stays honest).

## What LT measures here

pitch · moment precision · boundaries · reliability; the judge also checks the coach quoted the learner exactly.
