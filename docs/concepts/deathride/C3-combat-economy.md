# C3 Combat and economy — design first, 2026-10-01

Structure before coefficients. Preserve Combat as the only HP/wreck writer, bounded projectile/mine storage, fixed-step replay, insurance and idempotent race tickets. Add Scatter as a short-range spread attack with one deduplicated hit per target per activation, ordinary direction/range/road obstruction and a real ammo/cooldown cost. Four one-race items provide different choices: contact spikes, a straight-only turbo reserve, sustained fuel reserve, and a declared rival ammunition sabotage. They use the same movement/contact/weapon consumers, with no extra damage authority or permanent stat mutation.

Per-class hull capacity and utility slots are data. Light classes have less hull; Quill is the frail utility specialist. The first two career divisions reduce incoming combat damage for every entrant by the same authored encounter policy; difficulty changes skill only. Measure 2,000 actual races per early scenario using a declared Club reference driver, counting a lead-car wreck before its first completed lap as early. The under-5% requirement is a numerical gate; human fairness remains not measured. Later combat must still resolve and heavy hulls remain killable.

Persist ownership, condition, one-race inventory, outstanding debt, streaks and one optional contract in a versioned save. Old five/ten-car saves retain selected cars, installed parts, trophies and earned progress. Market transactions use a monotonic revision to reject duplicate/stale purchases, bounded debt and no resale profit. Buying a car may trade the current owned car but can never leave the player without one. Parts remain attached to their car. Loans are optional; repayment leaves a positive insured income floor. No compounding interest or loan-required progression.

The default pit remains fully insured. Optional manual service records actual condition, repairs in priced increments, and supplies a free minimum roadworthy floor after a result. Switching back to insurance does not heal the car before the next paid result. Contracts and clean/streak/destruction bonuses settle once with the race ticket; failure cannot seize the car or create a debt spiral. Cash pickups use bounded counters in Combat and the same result settlement.

Calibrate the first meaningful part toward 2–3 races and the first Club purchase toward 7–9 using actual shop transactions; C4 owns the complete multi-act money/PR timeline. Publish sensitivity/tornado and failure policies, not just successful spending. New commerce commands are structural menu operations with save-before-publish semantics. Minimal existing host/controller bindings are required to exercise core features; no drawing styles, rendering art, assets or art-directory work is included.

Required evidence: content assertions and invalid input/migration/duplicate tests, actual item effects and spread hit-dedup tests, fixed-step replay and allocation checks, 2,000-race scenario reports with the early-wreck metric, an insured economy sweep, full green build, /24 scan and a paired Stick shop/combat check. Record anything outside the measured scope explicitly.

## Results and calibration

Four weapons plus four utilities have actual consumers. Scatter resolves five directed rays with a target mask, one damage event per hit car, road clipping, ammo and cooldown. Spikes use the existing pair cooldown; turbo/fuel scale engine force while retaining the speed cap; sabotage changes ammunition once on structural reset and reports its target. Cash is bounded per race. HP is class-specific (76–100), while `Combat.maxHealth` remains the repair/HUD authority. Quill has three utility slots and a 76-point hull. These are mechanical changes, not new artwork.

The initial Crown diagnostic wrecked 39/40 lead drivers before lap one. The next coefficient still wrecked 10/40. Those logs are retained. Final campaign damage multipliers are 0.10/0.12/0.14/0.15/0.16 for the five divisions, shared by every entrant; practice remains 1.0. Accepted experiment: four scenarios × 2,000 actual six-car races = 8,000; all entries resolve, all terminal hashes are distinct within each scenario, no one-shot kills, and zero lead-car early wrecks. Crown retains 154 later lead wrecks (1,846 lead finishes). The lead is a Club-decision AI proxy, with field skills declared per scenario; this is not a human fairness sample or coverage of every loadout/course combination.

The independent raw-row audit verifies early-wreck flags against final HP/laps, sample and unique-hash counts, finishes, shot sums and mean times. Raw compressed rows, audit and logs live in `deathride/evidence/phase2/c3-accepted/`. Current allocation/replay tests exercise the new item and combat paths. Original W5 economy sensitivity gates remain alongside the new market sweep.

Market transactions are revision-guarded and save-before-publish. Fresh profiles start at zero cash with Line owned. Old v1/v2 saves retain their selected car, earned unlocks and installed parts as ownership, with repaired condition and no new debt. Trading clears the sold car's installed parts and discounts resale, preventing a buy/resell profit loop. One-race inventory moves into the issued race ticket at start and cannot be reused after settlement; aborted starts consume the packed reserve. The default pit remains insured, manual service has a 50% roadworthy floor, and selecting automatic service does not grant instant free healing. Debt never compounds; automatic repayment preserves the insured take-home floor.

The 2,000-path-per-scenario economic sweep gives baseline first engine 2.007 races and first Club purchase 8.6695 races, with no negative cash or loss of the last owned car. This preliminary model assumes positions 2–5 and 70–100 HP, not a physical outcome distribution; C4 must replace that assumption for the complete career timeline. Reward sensitivity shifts the first Club mean to 7.183–11.0055 races. Repeated last-place wrecks still pay but reach the first engine at race 5 and do not buy Club within the 24-race sample; that censoring is explicitly retained. Optional borrowing accelerates the first part but repays 330 credits on a 300-credit loan, and is not required by the baseline policy.

Minimal host/controller changes connect the core transactions, ownership gate, max-HP telemetry, weapon selection, encounter policy and result facts. No drawing styles, renderer art, assets or art-directory files change. The phone garage describes each contract's condition. New utility/pickup visuals and broader UI presentation remain P4/I1 work.
