What the labels mean

A technical pass requires decode, duration, loudness, true peak, silence and clipping screens, plus the seam screen for loops. Music: −14 LUFS ±1; voice: −18 ±1; effects: −20 ±2. True peak ≤−1 dBTP. The comparison copies use measured two-pass normalization with −2 dBTP processing headroom; dynamic limiting is disclosed per take.

Originals are unchanged and downloadable. Loop previews repeat three exact decoded cycles without a seam crossfade. The seam screen checks sample jump, boundary energy and spectral shape. A passing screen still needs a repeated musical listen.

Not measured: nobody has judged these in game. Masking, latency, Fire TV playback and memory, human preference, word fidelity, voice similarity, musical tempo/key/downbeat and long-race fatigue remain unmeasured. Rejected attempt · Provider request audit

Duration tolerance: music ±0.35 s, effects ±0.15 s, speech 4–15 s. Silence: below −50 dBFS for ≥100 ms; fail above 35% of a music/effect file or 45% speech, or an interior loop gap above 250 ms. Any decoded sample at |x|≥0.999 flags clipping. Loop screen: jump ≤0.02 full scale; first/last 50 ms RMS difference ≤3 dB; spectral cosine ≥0.80. These thresholds were declared before measuring; failures are not waived.
