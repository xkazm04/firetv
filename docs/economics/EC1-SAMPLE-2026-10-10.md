# EC1 sample, 2026-10-10

A measured sample of the Study Desk usage ledger (`desk/src/lib/engines/meter.ts`), taken live through the claude CLI on the subscription. The rows are in [ec1-sample-2026-10-10.jsonl](ec1-sample-2026-10-10.jsonl), verbatim, one JSON line per engine call. Price book: `desk/src/lib/engines/prices.ts`, asOf 2026-10-10.

## Method

- `node tools/econ-sample.cjs docs/economics`, run from `desk/` (live, claude CLI only, never in a gate).
- A fresh temp data dir outside the repo; `DESK_USAGE_FILE` inside it; `DESK_TEXT_ENGINE` unset; `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` deleted from the environment, so the subscription answered. Model: the fast one (`claude-cli/haiku`).
- Workload (13 claude CLI calls, cap 20): the three HL13 probe problems (a word problem, a Czech-notation equation, a derivative) each through `POST /api/hint` stage 1 and stage 2 with the real text provider, and the lesson pick (the route skips the library for a school or Calculus task, so where the route made no pick the script called `pickLesson` directly); then two practice sets through `makeItems`, `linear-one-step` and `calc1-limit-idea`, three items each.
- The embed and vision engines were stubbed; no embed or vision row exists in the file.

## Not measured

- Vision, embed, speak and listen were not live. The `mark` and `lesson-window` labels are pinned by the rules suite through stubs, but have no real rows.
- The Ollama vision provider's internal second post is not counted as a try.
- `read.ts` and every other unlabelled call site are unattributed (their rows carry `use: null`).
- codex and the voice providers (piper, elevenlabs, gravitone) are unpriced: marginal USD is null, not 0.
- Three problems and two sets are a sample, not a distribution; the input class is 2 per call because the CLI reports almost all input as cache classes.
