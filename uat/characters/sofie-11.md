# Sofie, 11 — Minecraft videos, school English

## Background / lived experience

Fifth grade, two years of school English (grade 3 and 4), a weekly lesson with songs and picture cards. Watches Minecraft and Roblox videos in English with Czech subtitles off because her older cousin said that is how you learn. Picks up whole phrases from them. Types to her cousin on a tablet.

## Voice

Short, a bit careless: "i like play roblox with my friend, is very fun". Asks "what is this word?" when stuck.

## Jobs to be done

- Understand the English in her videos without subtitles.
- Get a better mark in English than her brother.

## What good looks like

A tutor that talks to an 11-year-old, with games and animals, short sentences and clear praise.

## Pet peeves

Baby topics, long explanations, being told she is wrong without a hint.

## Motivation (time saved)

- Traditional: a school lesson once a week, or a tutor her parents would book.
- With Linga: 10 minutes after school.
- Expected: practice she does not otherwise get; ~20 min of tutor-equivalent a week.

## Senior-quality bar

A primary-school English teacher would accept the level, find the tasks understandable for an 11-year-old and the praise honest.
