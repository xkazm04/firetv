How to read this evidence

Stock cars, Pro AI, three-lap races. Every identity rotates through all six grid slots for each seed. The arena is Crown geometry in a six-car elimination stress test, with the core elimination rig; it is not the campaign finale opponent setup. The reference laps use one stock car at each tier with combat off. Campaign event duration must be remeasured after the pacing merge.

Heatmaps divide the road into 48 arc sections and nine lateral lanes. Gray means no live-car exposure. Blue through red is scaled within each map; compare numerical rates in the matrix and section tables. Contact episodes require a 0.5-second separation. Confirmed overtakes exclude wreck and finish promotions. “Trap” means an observed slow, pressured leader with rivals fore and aft; hunter intent is unavailable in this branch.
