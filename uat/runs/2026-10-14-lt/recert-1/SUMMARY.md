## Conflicts between Characters

- Scene pace: Petra (J3) says the partner kept adding requests past the natural close (LT-petra-38-J3-3). Klára, Viktor, Lukáš, Martin, Jana and Oksana say the mission ended too early. Both are closing-logic faults; not averaged. The close needs a rule tied to what is still open.
- Level feel: Ondřej and Martin say B1 is about right (Martin's was exact and quoted his errors). Petra, Klára (J2) and Tomáš say B1 is too high. Trust follows whether the label is backed by the learner's own quoted errors.
- Correction style: Adéla and Jana say corrections over-edit natural phrases. Martin, Oksana and Petra say too few errors are corrected. The shared fix is accuracy and coverage, not more or less coaching.
- Repeats: Lukáš found three repeats of one fixed sentence too many. Petra and Viktor want a replay with a fresh question. A fresh question serves both.

## Value ledger

Learners would use Linga for practice scenes: most said they would return (Martin, Petra, Jana, Oksana, Lukáš, Viktor; Tomáš only if the English is shorter). Time-saved claims run from -10 minutes (Adéla, who would re-check everything) to +60 (Petra, Viktor, as a replacement for an evening course). The gain is largest for work-focused adults with little time. It is lost for learners who want exact corrections (Adéla, Jana, Martin) and for A1 children and seniors, who get content above their level. Trust is the limit: a wrong level, a wrong correction or a recap line never said makes learners re-check everything.
