# Q1 — Debt, promotion and the parts counter

Design before code, 2026-10-02. Implement the Q0 rules as menu/career transactions outside the fixed step. A profile owns one campaign state with league principal, interest, paid/diverted/recovered/voided totals, last receipt, first-visit cursor, four reward states and reserved finale state. Optional shop borrowing remains in the existing loan balance. The combined deductions preserve the existing minimum take-home after insured repair. The conservation equation is initial league debt + interest - paid + diverted - recovered - voided = current league debt.

Race tickets still own settlement idempotency. First-visit interest cannot repeat after entering a menu twice, losing/retrying, reloading or returning to an earlier repeat-season event. A boss must be won before promotion. Ally joining and recovery of diverted funds belong to the same saved race settlement. Reward choice is a separate revision-checked transaction; failures leave it pending. Ownership, real part usefulness, legal tier caps and wallet headroom are validated before any reward is applied.

Version 5 adds a bounded campaign record to every profile, including explicitly empty NPC records. Decode all v1–v4 records with their existing migrations first, then initialize no retroactive league balance, no back interest and no duplicate promotion grants for previously cleared divisions. Copy, checksum, atomic-save and backup behavior retain the existing store. Save validation checks the ledger equation and finite bounded counters; malformed campaign data must not replace a valid save.

TV career shows league debt and last payment, with left/right reward selection and SELECT to claim while a reward is pending; DOWN can start without claiming, so the reward is not a race gate. The phone presents each available choice with the actual reward, rejection reason and ally text. It uses the same host transaction and revision as the TV. The parts counter shows the Mechanic from the start, an act-specific line and an optional tutorial on the phone. The full league ledger is visible there and in career. Existing portraits, backdrops, large type and phone target sizes are reused. No generation and no voice work.

Allied and hostile states are independent: a beaten boss can remember a wreck while staying an ally. Text selects the declared ally/grudge line; physics receives no friendly buff. Existing grudge decision parameters remain visible and unchanged. Finale persistence fields are reserved here; seizure and the rig are Q2.

Validation: exact ledger arithmetic and minimum-income boundaries, duplicate settlement/claim, all three reward paths and failed choices, exposure recovery, old saves, malformed records, pending choices through save/copy/reload, host/guest isolation, live browser rendering and the full required build. Synthetic fixtures are labelled as such. Balance and owner feel are separate Q3/Q4 evidence.

Validation: full regression {'core': 126, 'link': 3, 'game': 8}, required APK/desktop tasks green, followed by five targeted CampaignTest checks after a fixture-only correction. Four general browser suites plus the real promotion flow pass: all three rewards, host/guest separation, ledger equation and reload with Mica still pending. The real GL audit fits all 35 cards and passes missing-art fallbacks. Evidence is deathride/evidence/campaign/q1. The first promotion fixture lacked NPC funding and was rejected by preparation; its failure is preserved and the corrected fixture explicitly funds NPCs and calls the same gate before export. No gameplay exception bypasses that gate. Zero new images or paid calls.
