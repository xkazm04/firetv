## Definitions

Convex hull uses a monotone chain. Compactness is reported as lap length / hull perimeter and P²/(4π hull area); a thin ellipse can inflate the second, so both are displayed and neither overrides turning gates. Signed turn is calculated from centered secants on equal arc samples; three-sample smoothing, a 0.0018/m significance floor and a 12° aggregate floor suppress spline noise. Cyclic runs span the start line. A hairpin/reversal is 135–220°; a circle's continuous 360° turn is deliberately not called a hairpin. Kinks are <35°, sweepers have R≥6 L, and other significant turns are standard corners. Opposite-sign pairs/triples identify chicanes/esses with gap limits; double-apex candidates require distinct peaks and a substantial intervening curvature valley or a short straight between same-sign turns. These are reproducible candidate labels, not designer intent.

Turning signatures retain 256 signed increments. Absolute turn and significant left/right changes expose routes that merely follow their hull. Straight/brake pairs require ≥6 L before a ≥45° turn tighter than 6 L. This is an approach geometry test, not a measured braking input or passing event; the previous quality instrument retains its independent speed-budget passing-zone calculation.

Nonlocal proximity excludes arc neighbours within max(8 L, four local road widths), then counts samples with another segment sample within four road widths. Fold-back share additionally requires antiparallel tangents (dot product <−0.7). Distance is centreline-to-centreline and does not prove a barrier exists. Crossings use segment intersection including endpoint coincidences, deduplicated at centimetre resolution. Current playable format allows **zero crossings**: the renderer, projection and collision model have no bridge layer or route graph. R2 must define support or issue an explicit compiler diagnostic.

Pairwise outline distance normalizes centroid and RMS radius and minimizes Procrustes error over cyclic start, direction, reflection and rotation, on 64 equal-distance samples. Turning distance compares signed turns under the same kinds of transforms, normalized by 2π; its optimum is independent of the outline optimum. Similarity fires if outline distance <0.055 **or** turning distance <0.06. This deliberately catches rescaled, mirrored and renamed copies. It is a diagnostic threshold, not a guarantee of perceptual uniqueness or immunity to anisotropic stretching.
