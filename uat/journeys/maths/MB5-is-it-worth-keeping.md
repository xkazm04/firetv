# MB5 · Is it worth keeping?

promotion: discovery

## Goal (user's words)

"Give it a few evenings and tell me: does this actually help my kid, can I trust what it marks, and is it better than paying a tutor?"

## Definition of done

- On a first evening, the parent can set the child up and start the first piece of maths without a manual.
- Across the evening, the parent sees the desk hold back answers and mark honestly, including saying "not sure" when it is.
- At the end, the parent gets a recap in sentences: what the child did, how it went, and what needed help. A page for the week shows the pattern.
- The parent can say in their own words what the desk covers for their child and what it doesn't.
- The parent would choose to keep it, or can name the one thing that stops them.

## Levels

- **L1** (walker over code): the landing, the learner switcher and profile, Tonight, one MB1 or MB2 loop, the end of the session, the TV recap and the phone's Recap and This week. Read from `desk/src/tv/landingRows.ts`, `desk/src/tv/profileRows.ts`, `desk/src/tv/keys.ts`, `desk/src/tv/recapRows.ts`, `desk/src/app/phone/page.tsx`, `desk/src/lib/rules/week.ts`.
- **L2** (not runnable yet as a whole): the evening includes a snapped sheet, so it needs vision on local Ollama with the qwen 27B model. Without vision, L2 could still cover setup, a typed-answer practice loop, the recap and the week page.
