# R0 — Restart from driving decisions

2026-10-04. Read the complete master, T0, T1, session, merged campaign and geometry implementation; inspected the rejected 25-outline sheet. The stash is not applied. This note precedes implementation. It supersedes the earlier claim that radius entropy measures layout quality. Research below is public, original layouts will be authored rather than copied, and there is no art/audio generation.

## What failed

The old authoring process varied a closed perimeter. Almost every road stayed near its convex hull. Adding a radius, another polygon vertex or an inward shoulder does not make a meaningful new route. T1 could report several corner radii on a course whose entire lap still feels like driving around a block. Lengthening that perimeter for pacing magnifies the problem. The restart needs two independent proofs: a recognizable route structure, and races that actually work.

The current branch already includes AI pacing. There are now **29 course rows**, including four extended finals, and **35 stable events**, not just the 25 courses pictured in the rejected sheet. Five environment themes are industrial, quarry, desert, wetland and alpine; these are not the same as the five campaign divisions. Each existing slot must remain visible, with three alternatives except accepted Runoff, plus explicit boss/finale choices. Preserve event IDs and lap counts; report every assignment and tier. Do not silently equate an environment opener with a division opener.

## Width, vehicle scale and camera

Resolve W from the widest roster body (4.65 m) and L from the longest body (9.3 m). Typical smaller bodies are 3–4.1 m wide. The old 27 m road is 5.81 W by the conservative widest-car reference, **9 widths of Needle**. Always print both reference and metres; the owner's 7–10-car observation must not be hidden by using only the largest class.

Proposed technical road **3.6–4.0 W = 16.74–18.6 m**; ordinary connecting road 4.0–4.5 W; passing/grid exits 4.8–5.3 W = 22.32–24.65 m. Three W would violate the existing 3.6 W lint floor, so this restart preserves that floor. Widen before braking and retain width through release; do not widen only after both cars have committed to the same apex. Width variation is separate from scaling the course footprint. A final can be longer without widening its entire ribbon.

For context, a hypothetical 2 m wide real car makes Suzuka's published 10–16 m equal to 5–8 widths. Thus “real circuits are always 3–5 widths” is not supported. Three-to-five is our game design envelope, with explicit larger passing zones, not a historical fact about every reference.

At the current minimum 12.8 px/m, a 1920×1080 view covers roughly 150×84.4 m; at solo 17 px/m it covers 112.9×63.5 m. The longest body is 119–158 pixels and a 3.6 W ribbon is 214–285 pixels. A 23 m radius hairpin occupies roughly 63 m including road: its turn can nearly fill the short axis. The whole 500–1,000 m footprint cannot be readable on screen at once. A map thumbnail proves shape, not driving visibility. Current look-ahead is 0.8 s; at 35 m/s that shifts the view by 28 m. A 1.5 s warning is 52.5 m, so long braking approaches need landmarks visible before the apex appears. No camera or art change in R0.

Use a minimap for global orientation, a uniquely placed existing landmark for the next decision, and a stable road edge for immediate control. Avoid a bridge that hides a car at the exact point combat requires reading it. A top-down open junction must communicate which arm is legal and prevent checkpoint skipping. These are gameplay requirements, not labels in a design JSON.

## Measured layout archetypes and original planning envelopes

“Topology” here means spatial route structure, beyond the mathematical fact that most are one closed loop. `P/H` is lap length / convex hull perimeter; `P²/(4πA)` measures how much path is packed into convex-hull area. A circle gives 1 for both; a long thin oval inflates the area metric, so neither alone is sufficient. Measurements on our authored/baked routes are required in R1, not invented measurements on screenshots.

| Archetype | Spatial recipe and measurable planning envelope | Lesson and risk |
|---|---|---|
| Folded terminal | Outbound straight 12–22 L beside a return, 150–190° terminal turn of radius 2.5–4 L; separation 2R; add a different return-side sequence | Hairpin entry and exit pass; one fold is not three repeated ovals |
| Interleaved comb | Two or three unequal fingers inside the hull, 2–4 reversals; each finger 8–20 L deep; connect through opposite-sign bends | Braking rhythm; leave ≥1 W of ground between road edges and never let projection jump across fingers |
| Asymmetric kidney with infield | A fast exterior arc and a substantial interior technical excursion; P/H target 1.18–1.65, ≥4 significant left/right changes | Contrast speed with precision; shallow dents fail |
| Switchback ladder | Three alternating 140–180° terminals with unequal approach lengths; 2.5–4 L radius, linking straights 6–15 L | Repeated reversal with different setups; punish blind ice placement |
| Dogleg and compound return | Fast 15–25 L diagonal, 20–35° kink, braking hairpin, double-apex return separated by 3–6 L | Exit planning, not a rounded polygon |
| Folded crescent | Large sweeper surrounding a shorter inward hook; two different radius families, ≥1 150° reversal, interior length ≥20% | Hold speed then brake; inside/outside fighting lines |
| Street blocks | Unequal rectilinear excursions joined by 70–110° bends and one deep alley fold; ≥4 direction changes, no ambiguous unmarked junction | Landmark navigation and a short mine lane with escape |
| Unequal figure eight | Two different lobes with one explicit crossing; ≥1 hairpin or esse beyond the crossing; winding differs from simple loops | Spatial reuse. **Bridge requires height/layer collision and rendering; junction requires route-aware progress** |
| Split and rejoin | Two paths diverge for 8–20 L, one 5–12% shorter with grip/attack cost; shallow merge ≤30°, ≥4 L broad release | Choice that changes risk. A lane-band shortcut is not a second path |
| Arena with bays | Two/three unequal encounter bays joined by two escape approaches; wider local pockets with repair on an exposed side | Interception and escape, measured elimination duration rather than lap time |

Not every course needs every structure. No pair is accepted merely because it is rotated, reflected, stretched or named differently. Compare outlines after cyclic alignment, rotation, translation, reflection and uniform scale, and compare signed turning sequences as an independent measure. Three alternatives for one slot must differ in structure, not seed jitter. Runoff is the sole named grandfathered exception to shape gates; still show its measurements.

## Corner library in car units

All angles are signed accumulated heading change. Radii are **baked** minimum radii; Catmull–Rom overshoot means control-node radius is insufficient. Design with margin over the hard 2 L radius floor. Transitions need tangent continuity and enough samples; no tiny corrective segment to close a loop.

| Primitive | Dimensions | Sequence intent |
|---|---|---|
| Straight | Recovery 6–10 L; acceleration 12–25 L; 4–4.5 W, grid ≥4.8 W | A clear aim/recovery beat; a straight full of mines is not rest |
| Kink | 12–35°, R 5–10 L, ordinary width | One steering setup with limited braking |
| Standard corner | 35–130°, R 3–6 L, 3.8–4.5 W | Entry/apex/exit; reserve an outside recovery line |
| Hairpin | 135–200°, R 2.5–4 L, 3.6–4 W approach and up to 5.3 W release | Braking contest and switchback pass; don't stack compulsory oil and solids |
| Sweeper | 40–130°, R 6–12 L | Sustain speed and lateral weapon position |
| Chicane | Opposite-sign 30–65° pair; R 2.8–4 L, gap 0–3 L | Set up second apex; widen exit instead of both apexes |
| Esses | Alternating ≥3 turns 25–70°, R 4–7 L, gaps 0–6 L | Rhythm; aggregate nearby peaks but retain sign changes |
| Double apex | Same-sign 35–80° arcs; radius peaks separated by curvature valley and 1–6 L | Sacrifice first exit for second; don't count two random spline bumps |
| Tightening/opening bend | 60–140°, end radius / entry radius 0.55–0.75 (inverse for opening), minimum 2.5 L | Anticipate or release; cue before commitment |
| Width gate | Full width 3.6–4 W for 2–5 L; tapers ≥4 L where feasible | Short compression followed by release, never entire lap |
| Passing pocket | 4.8–5.3 W for ≥4 L, after ≥8 L fast approach and a ≥12% speed-budget drop | Two usable braking lines; confirmed passes in simulation |

Surface zones change grip rather than inventing camber. Use a priced inside lane and an asphalt alternative. Pickups should pull a driver away from the safest/faster line without requiring a collision; obstacle footprints retain their existing effects. Landmark warnings, pickup access and recovery gaps still pass the current linter. No new art is needed.

## Contracts for the next waves

R1: arc-resample the actual baked road; compute hull ratios, significant corner families and ordered turning signature, total absolute turn, left/right changes, ≥135° reversals, nonlocal parallel/antiparallel proximity, explicit crossings and straight/brake pairs. Require evidence beyond a convex perimeter: initial P/H ≥1.12, absolute turn ≥540°, ≥4 sign changes, ≥1 reversal, ≥3 corner families, fold-back arc share ≥0.03, ≥1 straight/brake sequence. Report P²/(4πA) with a minimum 1.2; this cannot rescue a thin oval. Require zero undeclared crossings. Pairwise normalized outline distance <0.055 or turning distance <0.06 flags similarity, with rotation/reflection/scale adversaries. These are conservative authored design gates, not psychological proof. Plant actual circle/oval/rounded rectangle/plus blobs, near duplicates and an illegal figure eight; prove all intended gates fire and retain positive complex controls.

R2: compile named primitives and width/surface/landmark/spot placements to the existing centreline CSV. Preserve Runoff exactly. The Lab must edit primitive recipes, preview actual core geometry and expose structural and shape failures. Split paths and crossings need actual semantics or explicit compile errors, never fake bridge labels. A safe compiler may reject unsupported bridge/graph routes; that is a disclosed remaining feature, not implementation completion.

R3: three genuinely distinct candidates per redesigned slot; role and event mapping explicit. Opening event winner-time target 120–180 s; finals 240–360 s (roughly double) and greater sequence complexity, with current laps. Measure six AI cars at the event's tier, combat enabled, many seeds and identity rotations; preserve raw results, finish/timeouts, overtakes, position changes, contacts, spins, stuck episodes, early lead wrecks, hunter-plan/contact corroboration, section heatmaps and reproducibility. Passing a geometric gate cannot erase adverse survival evidence. No universal minimum for traps that would force every track to be a trap.

Owner review shows outlines before art, road widths, ordered corners, gate failures, raw simulation scope and duration distributions. Keep/Maybe/Reject and notes use the audio report pattern with escaped Copy Markdown. No implied selections except the owner's existing Runoff decision. Render and inspect sheets personally before presenting a candidate set. Tests/build/browser green at each wave commit; final isolated Stick scan/check, pending if busy. No pushes.
