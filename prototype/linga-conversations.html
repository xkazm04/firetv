Choose a learner above, then select “Start talking” on the TV. The sample-reply control advances a scripted conversation; it does not record you. Arrow keys work when a TV action is focused.

01 / A reason to speak

The learner has something to accomplish. A clue, a follow-up question, or a disagreement gives their words a purpose.

02 / One useful change

The partner responds to meaning. At a natural pause, the coach helps with one phrase or communication choice, then returns to the scene.

03 / Try it somewhere new

Replaying with support is practice. Using a skill independently in another setting is stronger evidence. The learning map keeps them separate.
