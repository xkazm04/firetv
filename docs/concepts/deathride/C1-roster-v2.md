### Instrument review before acceptance

The first 40,000-race sweep used a 55% entry-rate check with three copies of each class. Its maximum possible rate was 33.3%, so it was not an informative dominance check. Retained those summaries as initial evidence and replaced acceptance with class share of race winners. The declared mixed schedule is 50% long-straight and 25% each technical/gravel, read from roster-courses.csv; individual course results remain separate. This is a content mixture assumption, not a claim about human track choice. It is stricter than the brief's literal per-entry threshold. Champion initially gives Kestrel 61.3% of this mixture, which fails. Increase Bulwark launch acceleration by one point while retaining lower acceleration than Kestrel and clear steering/braking deficits; rerun its four full scenarios. The other four tiers already meet the corrected mixture gate from their unchanged physical outcomes.

### Accepted physical sample

The acceleration-only correction overcompensated: its partial full sweep mathematically exceeded the 55% mixture limit. The final Bulwark has acceleration 9 and grip 7, retaining poorer steering, braking and launch than Kestrel. Its complete 8,000-race rerun passes: Bulwark wins 51.7875% of the mixture, loses 3.43 seconds on the technical course, and gains 15.36 seconds on the long straight. Champion-skill Kestrel beats Rookie Bulwark by 7.32 seconds on technical terrain. Other tiers' unchanged physical samples are reused, not rerun for cosmetic metadata changes.

The accepted set is 40,000 six-car races (2,000 per scenario, twenty scenarios), with all entries resolved and 2,000 distinct terminal hashes per scenario. All five pairs have a best and worst course type. Maximum mixed class winner share is 51.7875%. Twenty first-seed replays on the final code/data match the accepted raw rows exactly. Four-worker simulation uses independent worlds and serializes seed order; its 40-seed diagnostic summary is byte-identical to the serial run.

`deathride/evidence/phase2/c1-accepted/` contains compressed raw rows, summaries, independent audit, input hashes and logs. `tools/audit-roster.py --calibrate` fits positive derived-stat weights against the observed mixed inverse times under the authored PR constraints; the final PRs are all within 3%. The fit is approximate: for example Pro inverse-time ratios span about 0.947–1.059 while PR ratios are constrained to 0.970–1.030. Equal PR is therefore a useful summary with published residuals, not an exact equivalence or a combat score. Calibration emitted a SciPy/NumPy version warning; independently recomputed constraints and the JVM content gates validate the saved result. The optional calibration dependency is absent from the game and normal build.

The synthetic straight takes up to roughly 242 seconds, exceeding the playable host's 180-second ceiling. It isolates top-speed costs; it is not a career pacing claim.
