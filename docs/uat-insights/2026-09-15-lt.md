### LG-1 · Swaps forget what the learner turned down — build
- **Evidence:** 3 runs, 5 Characters, 10 rows (6 rated blocker). Here: `LT-adela-17-J2-1`, `LT-ondrej-16-J2-2`, `LT-oksana-34-J2-1`, `LT-petra-38-J2-1`. - **Voices:** *"Then the swaps kept returning things I had already rejected, and I left without agreeing a plan."* (Oksana, J2). *"I swapped photos and got cake, swapped cake and got photos. How many times do I have to say no?"* (Ondřej, `-lt-recert` J2). *"After seven changes I agreed because I wanted to stop, not because the plan suited me."* (Petra, J2).
- **Main today, verified in code:** a swap sends `avoid: k.topics.map(t => t.title)` (`lib/english/check.ts:192`). That list holds only the current titles: a topic swapped out leaves `topics` when the replacement is applied (`check.ts:220`), so the next swap may bring it back. The phone's Swap sends only the topic id (`english/LingaPhone.tsx:159`), so the learner cannot say what they want instead. *Hypothesis:* that these two facts explain the recycling the voices describe. The codex runs did not store the swap prompts.
- **Cost/value:** S. Keep the swapped-out titles and premises on the plan and send them in `avoid`. Optionally, let Swap take a few words of "instead". Every Character plans, so this is the widest early-path trust gap. Part of the lt and recert evidence is the no-goal confound (section 1), but the swap recycling survived the goal fix (`-lt-recert2-goal/LT-ondrej-16-J2-4`).
- **Ceiling:** a swap can still return a near-synonym of a rejected subject. Code compares titles, not meaning.

## 3. Methodology lessons

- **The driver's rendering lied twice, in the same way.** `VERIFY.md` caught the first-visit home that listed every situation. This drain found a second case: the recap. The judge read `Rehearsal saved: … N replies; N moments to keep.` (`uat/driver/linga-text.cjs:238` at 5e19b17e), while the phone at the same commit listed every moment with its fix and why (`english/LingaPhone.tsx:161` at bf2e0535). Since 3aa99c3a (2026-09-23) the driver renders from `lib/english/view.ts`, which lists the phone's moments (`view.ts:678-681`), so this class of lie is now structural rather than hand-copied. **Lesson:** a run's surface rendering needs the same import-chain audit as the L1 surface model before its findings are read.
- **J2 started from a fixture with no goal, in this run as well as in `-lt-recert`.** `recertify.md` attributes the generic topics only to the re-run that skipped J1. Every J2 here also began from `fixture: level set to … by hand`, with no goal and no interest. The 52% topic fit is a no-goal number too. **Proposal (overlay, not applied):** the J2 fixture carries the Character's goal, or the journey reaches the goal question.
- **One gap reads as many rows.** 69 open rows fold into 20 backlog entries and 5 closed-on-main items here. Across the five runs, 186 rows fold into 23 entries that carry rows (25 with LG-20 and LG-22) and 7 closed items. The ledger folds a gap only along `recurs` links, and no 09-15 run wrote any, so `recurring 0` describes the data, not the product. **Proposal:** a recertify that re-raises a drained gap writes `recurs` with the LG id.
- **Severity inflates per Character** (`VERIFY.md`). A single Character's repeated problem rates `frequency: high` and reaches rank 18 ("blocker"). The ranking here uses run- and Character-level counts.
- **Provenance-only checks pass semantic nonsense** (`VERIFY.md`), in the product and in the driver alike. This is the root of LG-16.
- **The voices carry what the rows strip.** LG-4, LG-7 and LG-8 rank by their voices. Tomáš's *"Then let me try by myself!"* and Martin's *"Show me the repeated mistakes"* are minor rows with trust-level voices.
- **Everything is codex.** All roles ran on codex `gpt-6-astra`, while the desk runs the claude engine. Every unconfirmed row is a lead, and the closed-on-main rows are re-asked under LG-22 rather than marked resolved.
