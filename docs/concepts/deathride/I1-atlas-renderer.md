Load the immutable `phase2-v1` catalog and libGDX atlases at presentation startup. Logical names and captured aliases resolve storage independently of gameplay. Failed pages, malformed metadata and absent regions are isolated; every caller retains its procedural representation. Cars require approved, shipped metadata: current references are all unapproved, so no car generation or candidate promotion is authorized. Implement runtime rotation around the recorded pivot, state selection and optional panel-mask tint without baking headings. Preserve physical dimensions and camera density.

Bake repeating ground, surface ribbons, shortcuts, barriers, props and oil/crack decals into one reusable 2048-square RGBA target (16 MiB, replacing 36 MiB). Keep procedural markings, collision geometry and their scale authoritative. Load world/UI pages only (8 MiB); autotile pages describe a grid topology the spline tracks do not use. Eleven repeating materials cost at most 2.75 MiB. One optional career backdrop costs 4 MiB; release it on replacement. Reserve 8 MiB for future approved cars. I2 will measure total memory, fonts and frame behavior and cut decorative effects if limits fail.

Dynamic pickup/effect/decal art reads reused presentation snapshots; never add sprite knowledge to core rules. Six-frame effects retain catalog timing/pivots, bounded pools and complete procedural failure paths. HUD frames use measured interiors, with existing readable text and bars retained. Portraits and a single current-act backdrop expose the story assets. No new images are needed by this design.

Validate actual GL loading/drawing, missing/corrupt asset fallback, region metadata, effect frame timing and APK packaging; then required core/link/APK gates. Scan the /24 before Stick checks. ## Implementation and observed limits

The final renderer retains the 16 MiB static scenery target but draws repeating roads and shortcut ribbons from cached quads at camera resolution. A Stick screenshot caught coarse road texels in the initial all-baked implementation; the live road layer corrects that without another texture. Procedural road marks remain at physical scale. Only world and UI atlases are resident (78 regions); both 47-case grid autotile families are deliberately unused by spline tracks. Aliases reconcile all six C4 rivals and the five `ash-*` story keys.

Snapshots now copy hull-relative health, wreck/flash/trace state, pickups and blast activations into fixed storage with read-only accessors. No atlas knowledge enters core. The full procedural fallback remains available, including cars, pickups, combat warning geometry and motion trails. Art particles use a fixed 96-slot pool, source timings and source pivots. Damage sprite keys require approved entries, measured body bounds and the requested state; an absent state uses a procedural damaged body. Optional tint masks affect painted panels only. No approved car page or tint mask exists, so those paths are wired but production-car behavior is not measured.

The HUD uses the actual hull cap (e.g. Quill 76/76), the measured frame opening, weapon/mine icons and warning-colored weak stat bars. Missing HUD art leaves all information visible. Current resident asset storage is 10.75 MiB, rising to 14.75 MiB for a story backdrop, plus 16 MiB scenery; total process memory and sustained budget acceptance belong to I2.

Validation: immutable bundle gate passes; catalog tests cover actual atlas IDs and all authored animation durations. The real GL audit uploads/draws 78 regions, measures visible pixels, checks backdrop eviction and isolates a missing catalog and failed pages. A complete no-art desktop smoke reaches a race. Core snapshot/allocation/replay tests remain green. Stick checks select all ten cars and all 25 courses and inspect story art, active driving and Home/resume; the readiness publication race caught by the first sweep is fixed rather than extending its timeout. Practice wrecks in these probes remain short; this is not a combat-fairness or G2 pass.

Grok spend: zero; 130/180 images reserved historically, 50 remaining, zero videos. All ten reference approvals remain false. No assets were regenerated or promoted.
