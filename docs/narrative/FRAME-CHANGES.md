# Death Ride: frame changes, a decision record for the owner

Head writer's proposals, 2026-10-04. Each row is one change to the campaign's story frame. You can accept or reject each one independently; the review page `deathride/narrative/review/index.html` has Keep, Maybe and Reject for every row. Nothing here has been applied to game data, code, art or audio.

**Cost vocabulary (as in K1):** **S** = text or data content plus existing presentation, with validation. **M** = a bounded new rule, menu, persistence hook or presentation trigger, with its tests. **L** = a new subsystem with migration and balancing. "Data" means the CSVs under `deathride/core/src/main/resources/data`.

**Recommendation:** **Adopt** means do it with the script as written. **Adopt later** means it is worth doing, but it needs a measurement or a system first; the script carries tagged variant lines so nothing blocks. **Reject** means it was considered and turned down.

## Owner rulings kept untouched

Every line in `lines.csv` was written inside these rulings. None of them is proposed for change.

1. **The owner's plot spine** (2026-10-02, verbatim in `deathride/campaign/OWNER-CAMPAIGN-CHOICE.md`): survival is at stake in paying the debt; a deeper twist about the money; winning each division's boss race turns that boss to your side as a medium of promotion (money, car, part upgrade); Marrow takes your car as part of the debt, the apex of his need for control; the Mechanic, the store owner, builds a very basic car with a mine dispatcher to end Marrow's reign forever; the last race is a fight to the death instead of a several-lap race.
2. **Promotion needs winning the boss race** (first place). DV1's "finish ahead of the named boss" was rejected on 2026-10-03, and every boss card says "win outright".
3. **Bosses have extended health** (`ai-bosses.csv`) **and target the player's weakest part.** The script never contradicts the behaviour. FC-16 only adds after-the-race lines, and those can be switched off.
4. **No visible hunt cue.** No line before or during a race announces a hunt or names a weak part. House-car barks react only after contact.
5. **Hunters target any leader duo and may wreck the leader.** House cars are the story reason (FC-05). No line changes the rule.
6. **The finale is a fight to the death in the Crown arena** (crown-7-a): last car running, 20-second opening protection, 600-second watchdog draw, free retries. The draw has its own line.
7. **The Mechanic builds a rough mine-dispatcher rig:** the fixed `mechanic-rig.csv` body, the automatic, speed-gated dispatcher, the 0.5 m Mine. Every mechanical claim in the script about it is true: five-second cooldown, drops only while rolling, mines hurt their owner.
8. **Marrow seizes the player's car** at the final preparation after event 34 (Q0); it is staged in the shop.
9. **Each division boss turns and pays money, a car or a part, and the player chooses** (`choosePayout=1`), with the amounts and named cars and parts in `campaign-allies.csv`.
10. **Q0 defaults:** `returnSeizedCar=1`, `duelEntrants=2`, `mechanicLoyal=1`, `allyRaceBenefit=0`. Allies stay rivals on track with friendly and grudge barks only.
11. **The 35 stable event ids**, the course picks (including scrap-7-e and the unchanged Runoff), and the **five regions with their owner-approved names** (Ash Yards, Cinder Row, Salt Cut, Thin Air, The Crown).
12. **Audio casting:** the announcer is Callum with the noir read; the Mechanic is Harry (diesel voice), a nervous, helpful young mechanic who runs the parts store. **All 14 recorded X3 lines are kept** ("Voices: no rejections there"). They stay in `lines.csv` as fallbacks (`recorded-keep`), or with a proposed replacement named (`recorded-replace`); nothing is deleted.
13. **Art decisions as applied** (14 keeps, 21 rejects, the original Ox, Relay and Marrow portraits). The script needs no new art. Ox is written as a woman, matching her kept identity.
14. **The 4 to 6 hour target, lap counts, economy numbers and stat caps** are unchanged. No line promises a number the game does not have.
15. **Original work only:** no franchise names and no lines from any cited work. The research dossiers' own example lines were not reused verbatim.
