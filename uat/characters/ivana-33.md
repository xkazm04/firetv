# Ivana, 33 — lab technician, a real B1 who freezes when tested

## Background / lived experience

Lab technician at a hospital in Olomouc. English at grammar school to the maturita, then a year of English series with subtitles and two summers working at a campsite in Croatia with Dutch and German guests. Reads English equipment manuals and safety sheets without a dictionary, follows a film without subtitles if it is not slang, and on holiday handles a hotel, a pharmacy and a broken car on her own. But any test makes her small: at the maturita oral she said three sentences and got a 3, and she has believed since then that her English is "bad". Under test pressure she writes the shortest safe sentence she is sure of and drops anything complex.

## Voice

Typed, short, hedged, mostly correct: "I work in a lab. It is ok." "Sorry, my English is not good." When she relaxes, one longer B1 sentence slips out: "Last summer I worked in a camp in Croatia and I had to explain the rules to guests."

## Jobs to be done

- Join the English training the hospital is starting for a new analyser vendor without feeling stupid.
- Find out what her level really is, because she suspects it is lower than her CV says.

## What good looks like

A calm check that does not punish short answers, gives her a level that matches what she can actually do in daily life, and tells her plainly what she did well.

## Pet peeves

Feeling examined. Timers. Being told she is a beginner when she reads manuals every day.

## Motivation (time saved)

- Traditional: a placement interview at a language school (free, but 30 min plus travel, and the oral is exactly the situation she freezes in) or a paid online test.
- With Linga: 10 minutes at home, alone.
- Expected: a level she can put into the hospital training form, without the oral.

## Senior-quality bar

An experienced adult-education English teacher would see the B1 under the short answers (correct past simple, 'had to', a because-clause, everyday vocabulary used precisely) and would not place her A2 for brevity alone.
