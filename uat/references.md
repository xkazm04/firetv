## How Czech speakers go wrong in English

10. Articles dropped or misplaced; present perfect vs past simple (Czech has no perfect); embedded-question word order ("I don't know what is his job"); false friends (aktuální ≠ actual, kontrolovat ≠ control, akce ≠ action); prepositions ("on the airport", "count with you"); over-apologising before speaking. englishcurrent.com/grammar/common-english-mistakes-czech-people/ · fluentwithellen.eu/false-friends-between-english-and-czech/ · tomczaban.com/2023/09/speak-english/

### The traditional way: time and money

20. **Teacher marking time:** "Marking one essay traditionally with a traditional mark scheme may take 5-15 minutes", about 5 hours for a class of 30 (No More Marking, a vendor of marking tools; fetched). help.nomoremarking.com/en/article/how-long-does-it-take-to-assess-one-classs-essays-using-comparative-judgement-lrxp0m/ · 8–12 minutes for a Year 11 essay (GradeOrbit, a vendor; *from search summary*). 21. **EEF, "A marked improvement?" 22. **Hattie & Timperley 2007, "The Power of Feedback"**, Review of Educational Research 77(1) 81–112: average effect 0.79 over 12 meta-analyses; praise 0.14; feedback about changes from a previous attempt 0.55 against 0.28. 23. **Parents' homework help:** the Varkey Foundation Global Parents' Survey 2018 (Ipsos, ~27,500 parents, 29 countries) found 6.7 hours a week on average; UK 3.6, US and Poland 6.2. weforum.org/stories/geographies-in-depth/indian-parents-are-most-likely-to-help-their-child-with-their-education/ (*secondary*; whether Czechia was among the 29 countries was not checked)

27. **Czech pupils:** Palacký University (E-Bezpečí; Kopecký, Voráč), 3 Dec 2024, more than 28,000 pupils: 28.04% of primary pupils have tried copying a homework from AI; 32.81% of pupils over 15 use AI for help with homework. e-bezpeci.cz/index.php/pohledem-vedy/4317-cesti-zaci-a-umela-inteligence-jak-ai-meni-vzdelavani (fetched) · Scio AI Kompas, Dec 2024–Mar 2025, 3,406 pupils and 776 parents in Czechia: 69% of pupils use AI regularly for school; 69% of parents view it positively; fewer than a third of parents rate their own AI knowledge as good. www2.scio.cz/pruzkum-ai-kompas (fetched)

### Czech school maths and its exams

31. **RVP ZV maths time:** at least 20 hours (grades 1–5) and 15 hours (grades 6–9) for Matematika a její aplikace; these are weekly hours added up across the grades, so about 4 h/week in primary and 3.75 h/week in lower secondary. Taught in every grade. digitalizace.rvp.cz/files/k7-rvp-zv-se-zmenami.pdf (*verified*)
32. **Přijímačky (CERMAT JPZ):** the maths test is 70 minutes, maximum 50 points (the Czech test is 60 minutes). prijimacky.cermat.cz/menu/jednotna-prijimaci-zkouska.html (*verified*). National maths averages: 2024 21 of 50 (42%), 2026 39.8%, January 2026 trial run 11.5 of 50 (*unverified*, snippets; the official aggregates on data.cermat.cz returned 404).
33. **Who sits it:** about 100,100 ninth-graders applied in 2025 (about 132,700 to four-year programmes); 94% of ninth-graders got in during round 1 (ministry figure 93.6%). ČTK, 15 May 2025, ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/2672097 (*verified*)
34. **Maturita maths results:** 12.4% failed the maths test in 2025, against 4.9% in 2024 (Blesk; Hrot24 gives 4.3% for 2024: the sources conflict). 19% chose maths, a third year of growth; the minister admitted the 2025 test was harder than usual. blesk.cz/clanek/zpravy-udalosti/813225 (*verified*). Over 15,000 chose maths out of 80,828 who sat the tests; a petition over its difficulty had about 9,000 signatures. hrot24.cz/clanek/maturita-2025-didakticky-test-matematika-studenti-petice-protest-obtiznost-HsfDy (*verified*). Failure by school type (3.7% at gymnázia, about a third at SOU, 47% in nástavba) *unverified*.
35. **Maturita maths format:** didactic test 135 minutes, pass mark 33% (17 of 50 points). statnimaturita-matika.cz/didakticky-test (*verified*, a tutoring site). Maths is chosen instead of a foreign language and is compulsory for no programme. maturita.cermat.cz/menu/maturitni-zkouska.html (*verified*)

### Parents, homework, tutors, marking

37. **Homework help:** "Domácí úkoly dělá pravidelně samostatně 46 procent dětí, dalších 45 procent je nejčastěji řeší s pomocí rodičů"; part of the parents "neví rady". NMS survey, August 2026, n=1,041; ČTK 7 Sep 2026, ceskenoviny.cz/zpravy/pruzkum-domaci-ukoly-dela-samostatne-46-procent-deti-dalsim-pomahaji-rodice/2871167 (*verified*). Varkey Foundation Global Parents Survey 2018, about 27,500 parents in 29 countries: global average about 7 h/week of homework help (Newsweek, newsweek.com/parents-emerging-economies-spend-more-time-homework-their-931748; *verified, secondary*; the 6.7 h of ref 23 *unverified* here). Czechia was not surveyed.
38. **Parents' maths anxiety:** children of maths-anxious parents learned less maths only when those parents helped often with homework (Maloney et al. 2015, Psychological Science, 438 first- and second-graders). sciencedaily.com/releases/2015/08/150810091859.htm (*verified, secondary*). A 2026 replication with 3,018 children found no such interaction; more frequent help predicted smaller gains whatever the parent's anxiety (Poisall et al., Developmental Science, doi.org/10.1111/desc.70261; *unverified*). Cite both.
39. **Maths tutor prices, Czechia:** 300–400 CZK an individual lesson; about 500 CZK an hour on average (about 600 in big cities, about 300 in small towns); students from 200, specialists over 1,000. doucsematiku.cz/kolik-stoji-doucovani-v-roce-2026-vyjadrili-jsme-se-pro-seznam (*verified*; no lesson length given). Superprof maths average about 337 CZK/h, 200–500 (superprof.cz/lekce/matematika/ceska-republika/; *unverified*). University maths 450 CZK per 60 min from one Prague tutor (*unverified*, snippet).
40. **Marking time:** Czech teachers spend 4.2 h a week marking (EU 4.9 h; gymnázium nearly 5 h, ZŠ 4 h). ČŠI, Národní zpráva TALIS 2024, csicr.cz/CSICR/media/Prilohy/2024_přílohy/Dokumenty/TALIS_2024_v5.pdf (*verified*). A maths teacher: 5 min a book, 1.5 min with whole-class feedback. my.chartered.college/research-hub/q-how-can-we-reduce-teacher-workload-without-affecting-the-quality-of-marking-a-whole-class-feedback/ (*verified*). No source found for minutes per calculus problem set.

### Answer-giving tools

41. **Generative AI and learning:** nearly 1,000 Turkish high-school students. During practice, GPT Base scored +48% and GPT Tutor +127% against control; in the unaided exam, GPT Base students did 17% worse than control (Bastani et al.). knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/without-guardrails-generative-ai-can-harm-education/ (*verified, secondary*). Khanmigo: "never gives you the answer", $4/month. khanmigo.ai/learners (*verified*). WSJ test: Khanmigo made basic arithmetic errors (343 − 17) and didn't fix them when asked to double-check. iblnews.org/story/khanmigo-struggles-with-basic-math-showed-a-report (*verified, secondary*)
42. **Photomath and copying:** Photomath scans a problem and gives a step-by-step solution, claims over 150 million downloads; a teacher: "Everybody's cheating all the time." fox5ny.com/news/cheating-a-free-for-all-at-virtual-high-schools-teachers-claim (*verified*). The repo's own market check (Photomath, Gauth: capture → solve in seconds) is `docs/STUDY-DESK-SCOPE.md` §1 (*secondary*).
43. **Reading handwritten maths:** exact-match rate for zero-shot GPT-4o is 50.61% (CROHME 2014), 46.03% (2016), 49.79% (2019) and 22.96% (HME100K). Gemini 2.5 Flash scores 55–58% on CROHME; the fine-tuned Uni-MuMER 71–75%. arxiv.org/html/2505.23566 (*verified*). A general model reads about half of handwritten formulas exactly; the local qwen model the desk uses was not measured.
