# J3 · My first conversation

promotion: discovery

## Goal (user's words)

"Let me actually talk about something from my plan, at my level, and learn one or two things on the way."

## Definition of done

- The Character starts a conversation from their plan and keeps it going for several turns.
- The partner's English sits at their band — not babyish, not over their head (rubric **pitch** ≥ 0.8).
- Any moment that stops the scene is correct and worth stopping for (rubric **moment precision** ≥ 0.9), and there are not so many that the scene stops being a scene.
- They finish with a recap and feel they practised something real.

## What LT measures here

pitch · moment precision · moment recall · boundaries · reliability
