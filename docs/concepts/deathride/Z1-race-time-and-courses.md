2026-10-04. Follows Z0 commit `86c3e76`. Sources and owner constraints are in Z0 and the parent AI pacing plan.

Measure each authored course with each of the ten stock cars and the four decision skills, two independent seeds per cell. Crown's lap measurement describes its road, not its elimination fight duration. Record length, curvature direction changes, surface transitions, authored features and physical obstacle count.

Before/after inputs and raw measurements are retained. Derive ordinary laps by rounding target seconds / reference lap seconds and clamping to 2–6. First visits target 150 seconds, ordinary level finals 300, intermediate visits linearly interpolated. The final elimination event retains zero laps and its own measured combat duration. Stable event IDs and save migration do not change.
