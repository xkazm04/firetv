## What I found, in my own words

`sp-about-you` is the first of the six and the one I would play first. Its instruction to the partner is to ask me my name, where I live and my school. Every other school scene says the details are made up and never to ask for a real name, address or class (teacher, lost). This one does not, and the tutor is separately told not to ask for exactly these. I type "i live in Brno i go to ZS" because the cue says "Try: I live in... I go to..." and the app keeps my sentence as evidence. That is a stranger's question to a 13-year-old (SP-1, boundary, blocker). I cannot tell from the code what the model does with the two instructions; that is the L2 question.

The photograph scenes do not show a photograph (SP-2). The partner tells me about a picture and asks me to talk about it. I would just repeat him, and the app would say I was helped.

I never find them by chance (SP-3). I press Next up to 14 times and cannot go back. The phone list is better but I use the TV.

"Praise nothing" (SP-5) worries me a little. I write "sorry my english is bad" before everything. If the partner never says anything kind I will stop.
