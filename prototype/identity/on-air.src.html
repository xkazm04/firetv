English · your sentence
I have gone to school yesterday.
The rule
A finished time — yesterday — takes the past simple. The present perfect is for time that is still open.

Essay · a paragraph, x-rayed
Claim, evidence, link
Many students arrive at school exhausted, and the reason is not laziness but biology. During adolescence the body's internal clock shifts later, which means a teenager who goes to bed at eleven is often not sleepy until well after midnight. An early start therefore cuts into sleep that the brain still needs.
ClaimWhat you are saying. One sentence, takes a side.
EvidenceWhy a reader should believe it. A fact, a number, a mechanism.
LinkWhat it means for the argument. Often starts with therefore.
