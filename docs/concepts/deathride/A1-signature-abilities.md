## The ten choices

Every class gets the same independently captured ABILITY hold. Holding repeats only when cooldown and energy permit; releasing prevents another activation but does not refund or erase a committed tell. All abilities have a visible wind-up, active interval and short recovery. Weapons are locked during those intervals, making activation a choice against existing Rivet/Hammer/Scatter/Mine use. Cooldown begins on activation. Energy recharges only outside those intervals. The HUD explains ready, arming, active, recovering, cooldown and insufficient energy.

## Budget, input and implementation contract

Each CSV row has `prAdjustment=0`: these are time/energy/weapon-opportunity purchases, not permanent free stat grants. PowerRating consumes that explicit adjustment through its existing authority; stock and upgraded cars use the same calculation. Quill's physical rebudget is checked by the existing tier tolerance and identity-pair lints. Zero adjustment is a calibration hypothesis, not evidence of equal expected strength. A3 reports actual winners; failed balance gates cannot be excused by the PR number.

Classic keeps separate GO and ABILITY; Cruise makes ABILITY also request GO while held, like its FIRE control, with BRAKE still overriding. Split leaves throttle on the steering pad. The whole right-side control group mirrors left-handed. Independent pointer ownership/capture and neutralization on settings, layout, blur, disconnect, pause and wreck include the new hold. Add no gestures to steering. Browser multi-touch proves wiring only; sofa comfort stays owner pending.

One immutable definition per class, assigned outside the step. Bounded per-car activation state holds phase, seconds, cooldown, energy, fixed aim/patch coordinates, activation counter and hit mask. Movement multiplies common engine/traction/steering limits without replacing CarSpec or allocating. Ability damage routes to Combat.damage with a new source kind. Non-stacking shocks keep the stronger remaining effect; no multiplicative pile-up. Wreck/finish cancels unfinished activations, so a dead owner cannot emit a later shot. Hash every state that can affect the next step.

Forward attacks use the existing three-circle ray and road-obstruction semantics. Position-resolved effects freeze their region when the cue starts; a renderer snapshot carries those exact coordinates. Arming budgets are checked against CSV perception allowance plus traversal distance at a declared reference speed, including car radius. The cue is both a spatial shape and a changing car/HUD state; audio remains absent, an explicit limitation against the registry's preferred visual/audio pairing. Owner readability is unmeasured.

AI requests abilities only on its existing skill reaction schedule, using bounded range/cone/road-visible perception and ordinary inputs. Burst requests require a clear local line; a lost opportunity is acceptable. Rules and costs are identical for humans and AI. No aim, HP or force bonus is tied to difficulty.

Presentation resolves `effectId` through the atlas when available and draws legible procedural tells otherwise. No generated art is required. TV and phone show name, energy and cooldown from the actual state; neither predicts success. Fixed snapshots and bounded geometry preserve zero step allocations.
