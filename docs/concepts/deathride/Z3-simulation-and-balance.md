## Results and deviations

The archive byte audit found 21 differing resources; 19 are explained by line endings, while two hashes still differ after normalization. The run therefore uses a fresh pre-change library from `ai-z1-before`, not an assumed identical archive. The final comparison includes AI, course/lap changes and their effects on the reference rivals' subsequent purchases.

The initial 768-fight duel pilot produced only 5 rig wins and 314 draws, versus 207 wins and 80 draws under the fresh pre-change control. A generic lane blend was closing Marrow's authored passing gap; preserving the duel's visible-target waiting maneuver raised wins only to 10/768 and left 365 draws. The long post-hit recovery window was also suppressing sustained fire. Named exploratory overrides, restricted to pilot directories, tested 20%/40% of the ordinary recovery and 110%/115% health, then 10% recovery with 110%/105% health. The selected candidate retains 105% Marrow chassis HP, scales duel recovery to 10% (0.12–0.14 s depending on skill), and preserves the passing gap. The 105%/10% pilot won 61/384 with 28 draws. Ordinary-race recovery and boss multipliers stay unchanged. Final 512-seed validation is separate; blocks 128–511 were not used for tuning.

The 5,760-race roster pilot resolved every entrant, preserved full slot/seed crosses and kept nine of ten class/build groups within 55%. Developed Quill measured 55.73%; this is retained as a pilot miss and checked with 334 paired seeds per six-slot cell. No chassis physics or ordinary-race temperament was changed on that small sample.

The skill instrument adds nine stock pairings on the declared technical course: five within-tier agile/fast pairs and four adjacent-tier agile-lower/fast-upper pairs. Every pair crosses both skill assignments and all six slots. The 3,456-race pilot passes the forward agile/lower-tier Champion margin and shows material improvement when skills swap. A stronger, additional requirement that Champion must reverse the finishing order in both directions fails for most pairs: on this track, agile Rookie chassis can still beat heavy Champion chassis. Report that diagnostic explicitly rather than pretending driver skill removes course identity. The larger final study uses the same declared selection rule and no physics overrides.

The full roster and ordinary campaign library were launched before the duel-only tuning finished. Their executing snapshots remain frozen. Final-core replay and a byte-difference allowlist must verify reuse: the only subsequent game changes may concern elimination recovery and Marrow's boss multiplier, which those non-elimination, non-Marrow-boss scenarios do not execute. Replays add no independent samples.

## Final measured results

The two fresh physical libraries contain 11,520 entries each. Successful lead exits have median 340.42 s before / 267.40 s after. Early lead wrecks are zero in both. After has 735 live timeouts (732 under-tier entries and three current-tier Rookie entries), versus one before; the shorter lap-scaled watchdog was not extended. All five opening stock/current-tier Club medians are 153.43–171.32 s; the four ordinary boss medians are 319.38–321.89 s. These are reference medians, not promises for every build: Foundry's opening p90 is 237.90 s. The finale is separately measured elimination.

Each version has two 2,000-career conditional cohorts. Race-aware completions rise 669 → 868, PR-buyer completions 8 → 905, with zero bankruptcy. After, Rookie proxies still complete none against the fixed Club reference. Successful Club/Pro driving-time medians are 4.465/3.885 hours for the race buyer and 4.400/3.775 for the PR buyer. Shorter individual races did **not** establish a shorter completed campaign; retries and the changing successful population matter. Censored runs remain censored at 70 attempts. All recorded boss promotions still require first place. Both after buyer policies miss the final ordinary boss's 1.03 first-entry power-ratio ceiling narrowly (1.031818); this remains an open balance finding, not a waived gate.

The separate 4,608-race difficulty cross resolves every entrant, keeps field/player PR invariant across difficulty, and gives matching Rookie/Rookie boss wins of 18.75%, 12.50%, 13.28%, 25.78%. Matching Club/Club: 24.22%, 15.63%, 7.81%, 27.34%; Pro/Pro: 44.53%, 17.19%, 7.81%, 22.66%. First-place wins remain achievable in this instrument, but human usability and a full Rookie-difficulty career are not proven. Ally payouts and promotion rules were not changed.

All 60,120 contested roster races resolve. Every tier/build has complete slots, paired seeds and hash diversity; all ten declared course-mix winner shares stay within 55%, and all peers retain a best and worst course. Developed Quill settles at 53.22%. The 6,000 independent homogeneous races agree with all fifteen contested timing orders. Final-core verification exactly reproduces 34 campaign rows and 360 roster rows, and the byte audit passes its narrow elimination-only reuse condition.

All 36,072 skill races resolve. Nine forward Champion agile/lower-tier margins pass (9.54–22.07 s), and every skill swap changes the mean margin by more than one second. The extra strict both-direction reversal passes only Flint/Vandal; the other eight remain explicitly reported diagnostics.

The final 3,072 duels produce 488 rig wins, 2,310 losses and 274 draws, versus 766/2,004/302 before. Each skill passes the existing 85% resolved-dominance ceiling, the under-5% early-wreck gate and zero one-shots. Held-out seed blocks produce 361 wins / 2,304 fights, 211 draws, and 82.75% resolved boss share. The final fight remains harder than before; it is not described as balanced at 50/50.

Across the after campaign library, hunters contribute 16.54% of impact-time leader damage; live hunt intent contributes 10.06%. There are 1,953,833 hunt decisions in 7,432 entries, a maximum of two concurrent claims, and median/p90 leader-rank changes of 1/4 (maximum 129, including close rank oscillation). This does not claim a uniformly tight pack.

Nineteen planted instrument cases and eighteen Python metric tests pass. Required release checks: 177 core / 8 link / 37 game tests, APK and six browser suites pass. Raw final CSVs and exploratory pilots are separately archived with hashes; immutable runtime manifests, failed runs, byte audit and exact replay evidence remain under `deathride/evidence/ai/z3`. Three SVG charts and `report.json` provide the review inputs. Z4 will expose the failures and limitations alongside the passes.
