Synthesised by claude-cli/sonnet. Read with report.md; findings are unverified at LT.

## Panel verdict

Linga is a good conversation space but is not yet trusted as a teacher. It places learners sensibly (5 exact, 4 near, 0 miss), holds every boundary (0 breaches), and builds topics and partners that feel like each learner's real life. Across nine Characters the same problems recur: it misses most clear errors (34 of 57), its focus and recap are generic, its 'with help' count is wrong, and there is no replay after a moment. Pitch is the other repeated problem, with children and A1/A2 learners getting prompts that are too long, and B1/B2 learners getting partner turns that are too easy. Fix the help flag, error coverage, replay and recap first, because each is reachable in a normal session and each makes learners doubt the numbers. Placement at the edges needs a method review, and swap needs a run where somebody actually uses it.
