# DV1 — The crews carry your name

Design before implementation, 2026-10-03. Apply DV0's 35-event / 367-lap proposal and finite tier model. `campaign.csv` owns phase and lap count; `career-curve.csv` owns targets and explicit bands. Early boss bands retain 0.85–0.90; late bands become 0.93–1.03, subject to owner review. This is a changed design contract, not a retrospective pass of the old 0.89 target. No chassis, stat cap, part price, weapon, ability or physics change is planned.

Ordinary boss promotion now means a surviving finish ahead of the named boss. The runtime and the report supply the real named rival position; missing rival evidence keeps the old first-place requirement. The whole-field finishing position still owns prize and cup points. An ordinary wreck can qualify for progress but cannot earn boss promotion. No retries auto-complete a boss. The death duel retains last-car-running victory, supplied rig, free retry and saved seizure/restitution.

Rook's promotion gives public access to crew buyers; Ox's brings receipts and recovered stolen funds; Vex's brings a courier route and an unedited recording; Mica's turns isolated evidence into a public network that cannot be destroyed by one wreck. The Mechanic's secrecy protects the sibling rather than teasing a random betrayal. The dispatcher begins as a scrapped industrial mechanism, then a visible workshop project, then a disclosed last chance. Marrow knows the cash claim is gone and openly replaces it with ownership. The last fight is an attempt to silence the crews, not another invoice.

Cash choices retain 350/650/1,000/1,400 CR. A chassis choice first offers the named car, then the missing peer in that tier if the named car is already owned. A part first offers the ally's specialty, then another useful unlocked part on the selected owned car. Labels show the exact asset before claiming. A complete tier garage or a fully developed selected car truthfully refuses that option; choices can remain pending. They are not promised equal monetary value, and a capped wallet never silently consumes an unavailable grant.

On exposure, stolen money first reduces principal. The rest becomes restitution due. Wallet space collects that credit on campaign preparation; overflow remains due. `diverted = recoveredAgainstPrincipal + restitutionPaid + restitutionDue` after exposure; the original principal equation is unchanged. No money is created twice by a repeated receipt, menu visit or restart. Save v6 appends two campaign counters; v5 gains only its previously unrecovered stolen-money entitlement, never a new debt or duplicate promotion. V1–v4 retain existing no-retroactive-debt migrations. Stable event IDs avoid remapping active careers.

Core tests will cover real boss identity/position, surviving finish, replay, no allocation in the fixed step, v5/v6 and earlier save paths, wallet overflow, duplicate transactions, every payout alternative, loss-only roadworthiness and unrestricted retry. TV/phone text exposes the objective and restitution. New story text has no new voice recordings; existing narration remains a brief cue, not a claim to speak every revised line. Art and sound assets are unchanged.

Full fresh balance evidence belongs to DV2. All Stick and owner feel checks remain pending.

Save v5 fixture migration, idempotent receipts, full-wallet restitution, alternate reward usefulness, named-rival standings and 500 loss-only settlements pass alongside existing active-step allocation and replay checks. That failed observation is retained and desktop retries are pending, not counted as a game success or a device result.
