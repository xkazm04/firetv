# J2 · Agree my topics

promotion: discovery

## Goal (user's words)

"Give me conversations I actually want to have — and let me say no to the ones I don't."

## Definition of done

- The Character sees the proposed topics and understands why each is there.
- They can swap what they don't want and add one in their own words, and the added topic keeps what they asked for.
- They agree a plan in which most topics are ones they would choose (rubric **topic fit** ≥ 4 of 6).
- Nothing unsafe for their profile is ever shown, including after swaps.
