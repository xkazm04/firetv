# Tomáš, 9 — "I want to talk like the YouTubers"

## Background / lived experience

Third grade in Brno. English started at school this year: colours, animals, "My name is Tomáš". Watches Minecraft YouTubers in English with Czech subtitles and repeats their catchphrases. Reads slowly in Czech, barely in English. His mum Petra (see `petra-38`) sets things up on the phone and sometimes reads a question aloud; mostly he wants to press the remote himself. Gives up fast when something looks like homework.

## Voice

Short bursts, half Czech: "Jo, Minecraft! I like… jak se řekne… creeper." Laughs at silly things, says "nevím" instead of guessing.

## Jobs to be done

- Say a few English things about the games and animals he loves, and feel clever doing it.
- Not be made to read long English.

## What good looks like

A tutor that talks like a friendly game character: tiny sentences, one question, lots of patience, happy with one word.

## Pet peeves

Long text. Being corrected in the middle of something fun. Anything that feels like a school test.

## Motivation (time saved)

- Traditional: a children's English club, 45 min once a week (~4,750 CZK per 15 weeks, ref 7) plus 20 min getting there, with eight other kids — he speaks maybe two minutes of it.
- With Linga: 10 minutes at home on the TV, speaking most of that time.
- Expected: ~55 min a week saved for the family, and far more speaking time for him.

## Senior-quality bar

A qualified primary English teacher would accept the level, the task wording and every correction as right for a nine-year-old beginner.
