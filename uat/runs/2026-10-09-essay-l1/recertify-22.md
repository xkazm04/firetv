## The rule (round 5, a non-word-list signal)

`desk/src/lib/rules/essayLang.ts` is a pure-TypeScript character 1-4-gram profile with no dependency. For each letter run of a folded, space-padded word it holds the smoothed log odds of Czech over English (smoothing 0.1, runs seen twice or more, 3380 runs). `trainProfile()` trains it on tools/essay-tuning-czech-short.json, essay-heldout-czech-short.json, -r2, -r3, -r4 and essay-heldout-r3.json, and **never on r5**. The embedded `PROFILE` is that output. The test `L1-helena-46-EM1-9 round 5` rebuilds it from the files and fails on any drift, so nobody can tune the table by hand. **No word or shape was added to any list.**

`paragraphIsEnglish` (rules/essay.ts:305-313) reads the profile on paragraphs of up to 12 words, in two ways:

- **Lifting a refusal.** A refusal that rests on Czech alone is lifted when the line leans English by more than 0.15. A refusal another language makes is never lifted.
- **Adding a refusal.** A line of 3 or more words with no English function word is refused when it leans Czech by more than 0.2. Two-word lines stay with the word lists: no English training line is that short, and the pinned "Slavoj Žižek" stays English.

Both margins were chosen by leave-one-set-out cross-validation over the five short training sets: train on four, test on the fifth. They sit in the middle of a plateau where the cross-validation made 0 errors: 0 Czech leaks and 0 English refused, over 288 Czech and 220 English lines.
