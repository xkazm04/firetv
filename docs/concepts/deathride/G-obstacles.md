## Wave G1: physical natural obstacles

Core CSV definitions preserve the selected local-metre ellipse footprints, visual extents, heights and shadow parameters. Explicit per-course placement rows contain a definition, centreline fraction, lateral metres, heading offset and seed. Seeded heading/position variation is baked once at course construction, independent of the race seed; no random draws or allocation in the 60 Hz step. Every course has a bounded sparse set, away from its grid and racing line. Decoration uses effect `none`; the shadow never participates in physics. Rubble can use the rock-field art with an explicitly separate drag definition.

Solid contacts use the car's existing three-circle hull, the shared inverse-mass impulse equation with an immovable second body, depenetration and bounded yaw. Normal restitution and tangent speed loss bleed speed; existing wall damage/protection/cooldown rules apply, without awarding a ram kill on scenery. Drag applies continuous time-based resistance inside the ellipse, with the art's 0.68 retained as a one-second velocity multiplier, not a per-frame multiplier. Overlap takes the strongest drag, never multiplies every patch. None affects neither motion nor perception.

AI sees only forward obstacles within the authored perception range and an unobstructed road sightline, at its existing reaction cadence. It steers toward a clear interior lane; recovery still uses ordinary controls. Solid footprints occlude shots and harpoons through the existing sightline/ray path. Mines cannot be laid inside solids; brush does not absorb them. Rams cannot destroy or farm scenery, shields keep their existing damage semantics, and harpoons still target cars only. No teleport, extra AI grip or obstacle immunity.

The linter checks definitions/placement bounds, minimum contiguous usable road width (existing car-width authority), a car-width corridor along both normal and optional shortcut racing lines, and full grid hulls plus clearance. It evaluates conservative footprint bounds across the road segments, including between authored nodes and across the start seam. Each rule gets an invalid mutation that must fail. The renderer uses the same baked placement and atlas logical ID: shadows and low props below cars, tall crowns above cars, procedural silhouette fallback at the same footprint.

Acceptance instrument: paired obstacle-on/off races over all 25 courses and five tier pairs, independently crossed six grid rotations, seeded Club drivers and fixed combat rules. Report finish times, watchdog survivors/stuck cars, wrecks and wrecks before lap one; winner share means wins divided by races, never entries. Compare each tier/course/rotation cell and retain any regression. A 99% distinct-state-hash alarm, complete rotation cross and planted dominance/constant-hash/unresolved/early-wreck cases must prove the instrument can fail. Exact replay and allocation checks cover active obstacles. Owner visual/feel checks and Stick performance stay pending.

The other 24 courses use the delivered tree, boulder, brush, dune and rock-field footprints, including a separately named rubble drag variant. Placement jitter is fixed per authored row; lap and race resets do not move props. Tall crowns render above cars, while footprints and shadows remain below them. The first three exploratory cohorts exposed persistent shoulder traps.
