## How Czech speakers go wrong in English

10. Articles dropped or misplaced; present perfect vs past simple (Czech has no perfect); embedded-question word order ("I don't know what is his job"); false friends (aktuální ≠ actual, kontrolovat ≠ control, akce ≠ action); prepositions ("on the airport", "count with you"); over-apologising before speaking. englishcurrent.com/grammar/common-english-mistakes-czech-people/ · fluentwithellen.eu/false-friends-between-english-and-czech/ · tomczaban.com/2023/09/speak-english/
