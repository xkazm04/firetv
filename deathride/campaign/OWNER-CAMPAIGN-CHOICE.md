# Owner campaign choice (2026-10-02)

Triage page: `deathride/campaign/directions/index.html`; design note `docs/concepts/deathride/K1-campaign-directions.md`. Quoted verbatim, then the host's reading (marked as the host's).

## The owner's words

> Campaign - 01 with direction survival is in stake to pay the debt, trying to think up with deeper plot twist what happens with the money - winning with each round final boss would turn it to your side, using them as medium for promotion (money, car, part upgrade). Key plot for the final duel is in taking player's car as part of the debt and apex of Marrow's crazy need of control. 'The mechanic' (store owner) will though implement in very basic car mine dispatcher with goal to end Marrow's reign forever - last race will become then fight to the death instead of standard several laps race

## Host reading (not the owner's words)

- **Direction 01, the Ash Circuit**, as the base: a debt owed to Marrow, who owns the league; survival is at stake, not only money.
- **Where the money goes** (the twist the owner asks to think through): the player's winnings pay down the debt, but Marrow controls the books. Winning each division's boss race **turns that boss to the player's side**: the beaten boss becomes an ally who acts as a **medium of promotion**, paying out in **money, a car, or a part upgrade** (the player picks which, or the ally's character decides). So progression flows through defeated rivals, not only prize money.
- **Final act**: Marrow **takes the player's car as part of the debt**, the apex of Marrow's need for control. **The Mechanic** (the parts-store owner, voiced as the nervous, helpful young mechanic, see `deathride/audio/OWNER-AUDIO-CHOICE.md`) has been secretly building a **very basic car with a mine dispatcher** (a rough rig that lays mines) whose aim is to **end Marrow's reign for good**. The last race is a **fight to the death, not a standard several-lap race**.
- **Mechanics implied** (to be designed, with cost): boss-turn ally rewards (a post-boss choice and a changed relationship); a debt meter and ledger; the Mechanic as the shop NPC from the start; a story beat where the player's car is seized; a special final car with an automatic mine dispatcher (the existing Mine weapon, blast radius 0.5 m, and the ability system); a death-duel event type (elimination, no lap win; last car running wins; Marrow's AI and car as the apex opponent).
- Original content only; nothing from the arcade game or the Death Rally titles.
- Open questions for the owner, to confirm during design, not blockers: does the player choose the ally's payout or does it vary by ally; is the final car fully replaced or does the seized car return as a reward; is the death duel one-on-one or with the field; how does the Mechanic's betrayal risk (or loyalty) show.
